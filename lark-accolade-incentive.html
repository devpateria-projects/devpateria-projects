Why This Programme

Built for those who
think in crores.

Accolade isn't about the count of applications — it's about the quality and size of business your team originates. This programme is exclusively for large wealth firms with RM/Advisor-led private wealth practices.

Every RM is measured individually. Multiple RMs within the same firm can qualify and earn rewards independently in the same month — creating a genuine performance culture that rewards every high-performing relationship manager.

Unlike conventional application-counting programmes, Accolade rewards your team on total loan volume sanctioned each month — meaning high-ticket clients directly translate into higher-tier rewards. The bigger the book, the better the prize.

How It Works

Five steps to
your reward.

Designed for simplicity. Every RM participates independently using their own unique customer link.

01
Add Your Team
Log in to the Lark Partner Portal and add all your RMs, Advisors, and Sub-Brokers under the Manage Team section.
02
Activate Unique Links
Each RM receives a unique LAMF customer onboarding link. Customers must register only through their assigned RM's link.
03
Originate Loans
Customers complete their journey and receive sanctioned LAMF limits. Minimum ₹5 Lakhs per application. New limits only.
04
Hit Monthly Slabs
Each RM's cumulative sanctioned volume is tallied monthly. Slabs reset each calendar month — a fresh opportunity every 30 days.
05
Collect Your Reward
Winners announced in the first week of July. Rewards settled by 30 August 2026 — fully over and above your LAMF trail income.

Eligibility

Who
qualifies?

Accolade is designed exclusively for large wealth firms with an RM/Advisor-led private wealth business. Each RM participates at an individual level.

Important Note
This is an RM-level programme, not an organisation-level programme. Multiple RMs within the same firm can qualify and earn rewards independently in the same calendar month — creating a true performance culture across your entire team.
  • ✓
    Active Lark Partner
    Must be an officially onboarded partner on Lark Finserv's Partner Portal with an active account.
  • ✓
    RMs Added via Partner Portal
    All participating RMs/Advisors/Sub-Brokers must be added under the Manage Team section of the Partner Portal before originating loans.
  • ✓
    Minimum 10 Applications Per Month
    Each RM must have a minimum of 10 fully sanctioned applications in the calendar month to qualify for any reward slab.
  • ✓
    Minimum Ticket Size of ₹5 Lakhs
    Every qualifying application must carry a sanctioned loan limit of ₹5,00,000 or above to count toward monthly volume.
  • ✗
    New Limits Only
    Enhancements, renewals, margin call responses, and limit increment requests are not eligible. Fresh sanctions only.
  • ✗
    Unique RM Link is Mandatory
    Customers must register via the RM's own unique link. Loans not mapped to the RM on Lark's portal will not be counted.

Terms & Conditions

Programme guidelines.

Please review carefully. Participation in Accolade implies acceptance of all conditions listed below.

01
All applications must have a minimum ticket size of ₹5 Lakhs to be counted toward monthly volume.
02
Only newly opened loan limits are eligible. Enhancements, renewals, and limit increments are excluded.
03
Rewards are calculated on both the number of applications and total sanctioned volume on a monthly basis.
04
All RMs/Advisors/Sub-Brokers must be duly added in the Manage Team section of the Lark Partner Portal prior to originating loans.
05
Only customers mapped to the RM on Lark's portal will count as that RM's cases. Unmapped customers are excluded regardless of referral.
06
Each RM must use only their own unique customer link to ensure proper customer mapping on Lark's platform.
07
This is an RM-level programme. Multiple RMs in the same organisation can independently win rewards in the same month.
08
All Accolade rewards are over and above the regular LAMF trail income and processing fee payouts earned by partners.
09
Winners will be announced in the first week of July 2026. Rewards will be settled by 30 August 2026.
10
A minimum of 10 sanctioned applications per RM per month is mandatory to qualify for any reward slab.
11
Only loans fully sanctioned by 30 June 2026 will be counted. Pending or in-process loans will not be included.
12
Lark Finserv reserves the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue this programme at any time with reasonable prior notice.
